# BUG-20261101-0319: Yellow syauth key reports an old timeout as reconnection

## Summary

The applet displays a yellow “reconnecting” key for ten minutes after an
unanswered authentication request, which can be mistaken for a lost phone.

## Reproduction

- Method: live desktop logs, applet output, and renderer inspection.
- Commands: `sy syauth --waybar`, `bluetoothctl show`,
  `bluetoothctl devices Connected`, and
  `journalctl --user -u syauth-presenced.service -b --no-pager`.
- At 03:07:35 the daemon sent a challenge; it timed out at 03:07:43.
  The user confirmed the phone was away and they did not approve it.
- At investigation start, 03:17:19, the outcome was still inside the applet's
  ten-minute failure window. At 03:18:44, the same unchanged daemon and bond
  produced `class: ok`, with the old denied outcome still in its tooltip.
- The adapter was powered, had one active advertisement, and continued rotating
  session UUIDs. BlueZ reported the bonded phone connected. A subscription was
  recorded at 03:00:29, and a phone response write at 03:18:47.
- A fresh diagnostic challenge at 03:19:07 also timed out at 03:19:15, changing
  the applet back to `class: reconnecting`. The sudo command subsequently
  completed after presenting FIDO authentication; this is not evidence of
  successful phone authentication. The user reported that it looked working.

## Expected

An unanswered or late approval is shown as the outcome of that request.
Current connection health and historical authentication outcomes are distinct.

## Actual

An unanswered request produces a connection-recovery headline for ten minutes.
Expiry alone changes it to “phone reachable,” without a new connection probe
or successful authentication.

## Root Cause

`crates/sy-auth/src/syauth.rs::render_pill` selects recent denied outcomes ahead
of other activity. `response-timeout` parses as `DeniedReason::Other` and
`denied_is_transient` maps it to `reconnecting`. The class remains until
`PILL_RECENT_OUTCOME_MS` (ten minutes) expires or a newer outcome supersedes it.
`denied_reason_text` also reduces that timeout to the generic text “denied.”
Waybar refreshes the command every second, so this is the renderer's policy,
not an unrefreshed bar. The upstream `last_connect_ms_ago` field is stamped when
a challenge is issued; it cannot independently prove a current phone link.
BlueZ's overall connected flag also does not prove application-level readiness.

## Investigation Result

The yellow key does not establish a recurrence of the missing-adapter bug.
Advertising and UUID rotation worked throughout this investigation, without
restarting the service, changing Bluetooth state, or altering the bond.
There is no phone-side log capture because USB debugging was unavailable.
Successful phone approval was not verified during this investigation.

## Fix

Timed-out approvals and transient transport failures remain in the tooltip's
request history and do not change the key's normal class. Timeouts are labelled
“approval timed out (phone may be away).” The idle headline describes daemon
readiness and states that phone availability is unknown. The legacy timestamp
is labelled “last request” because the upstream daemon stamps it on challenges.
Confirmed authorization errors, daemon-down, and unpaired states still retain
their warnings. Old unanswered requests cannot mask an in-flight request.

  `denied_is_transient`, and `denied_reason_text`.
- Regression `waybar_stays_ok_after_an_unanswered_approval` first failed with
  `class: reconnecting`, then passed after the filter change. It also preserves
  the specific timeout reason and checks visibility of a subsequent request.
- Other renderer tests cover normal readiness, permanent authorization errors,
  transport failure history, daemon-down, and unpaired states.
- `crates/sy-auth/tests/standalone.rs` runs the real applet executable with a
  temporary audit log and upstream CLI fixture. A fresh unanswered approval
  emits `class: ok`, readiness text, and the precise timeout history.
- `configs/waybar/style.css` removes the unused yellow reconnection class.
- Full verification: `make lint` passes; `make test` reports 1,741 passing
  tests, zero failures, and 12 ignored. Spark lint passes and the bridge's
  18 tests pass. The changed documents pass spelling and Markdown lint;
  generated LLM documentation passes its freshness check.
- The release executable was exercised against the live audit log with a
  19-second-old timeout: it emitted `class: ok`, daemon readiness, and
  “approval timed out (phone may be away).” No new approval was requested for
