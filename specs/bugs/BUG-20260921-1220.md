# BUG-20260921-1220: cold-start recovery exceeds the service deadline

## Summary
Migration rollback restored the source state but the source agent was repeatedly
killed while waiting for the large model to load, exhausting its restart budget.

## Reproduction
- Live recovery of the v0.1.3 transaction on 2026-09-21.
- `systemctl show sy-spark-agent.service -p TimeoutStartUSec -p NRestarts`
  reported 90 seconds and six restarts; the journal recorded startup timeouts.
- The source container was subsequently removed after restart suppression.
- Regression: `tests/migration_fence.rs::model_warmup_fits_inside_agent_startup_deadline`
  failed before the service unit change.

## Expected
Initial reconciliation has time to finish the configured model cold start.
Recovery remains possible after an explicitly approved managed replacement.

## Actual
During the failure, inference was unavailable and the transaction remained fenced.
Ordinary managed recovery clears suppression by creating generation 9, but the
original recovery executable requires generation 8 and its own exact hash.

## Root Cause
The notify service inherited systemd's 90-second startup deadline while the
engine's startup budget is 1800 seconds. Recovery lacked a signed handoff for
an updated recovery executable or an explicitly authorized replacement generation.

## Fix
Allow 1900 seconds for startup, including during source recovery. Require the
installed authority to sign the exact recovery executable, immutable transaction
record, host, expiry and exact replacement container identities. Permit such a
handoff only after state restoration, before reopening traffic. Preserve the
original record and perform normal health checks; never edit generations in the
database, relax admission, or bypass the traffic fence.

## Traceability
- Sparkplane service unit, migration fence and signed recovery tests.
- Live managed generation 9 requested after admission reported zero swap-in and PSI.

## Resolution

PR #7 passed required CI and released v0.1.4. The original authority signed the
exact replacement, recovery executable and unchanged transaction record;
`--recover` returned success and reopened traffic. A fresh v0.1.4 migration then
completed its full acceptance suite and committed with zero new-service restarts.
No journal/database edits or admission bypass were used. The final running model
retains context 262144 and measured 33.804 decode tokens/s versus 33.808 before.
