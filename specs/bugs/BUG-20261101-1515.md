# BUG-20261101-1515: Inconsistent Waybar backgrounds and hover feedback

## Summary

Top-bar modules should have black backgrounds at rest and the same slightly
brighter background on hover, including power, clock and workspace buttons.

## Reproduction

- Move the pointer away from the power button and clock: both retain a brighter
  background. Hover CPU, memory, battery or authentication: they lack the shared
  highlight used by network and other controls.
- The supplied screenshot shows the permanent power-button background.
- Render `waybar/style.css` with sy and inspect it through GTK 3: power and
  clock retain `@bg1` in the normal state. An isolated GTK check across configured
  modules and their status classes found 108 inconsistent normal/hover colors.

## Expected

Every module and individual workspace button uses the theme's black background
at rest and `@bg1` on hover. Focus, active and urgent states do not introduce
different backgrounds. Status foreground colors remain useful.

## Actual

Power and clock are permanently bright, some modules never highlight, and
urgent workspace buttons use a red background.

## Root Cause

`configs/waybar/style.css` mixes permanent per-ID backgrounds with an incomplete
list of hover selectors. The bar's base background is also lighter than black.

## Fix

Use shared module and workspace-button background rules. The workspace module
is a container: highlight each workspace button rather than the entire group.
Remove permanent power/clock backgrounds and the urgent workspace fill; retain
urgent foreground and border indicators. Apply the same base to the legacy
cheatsheet bar.

## Traceability

- Source: `configs/waybar/style.css`.
- Verification: load sy's rendered CSS in GTK 3 and check normal and prelight
  colors for every module listed in the top-bar configuration, each declared
  status class, and normal/active/focused/urgent workspace buttons.
- Live check: move the pointer over and away from power, clock, workspace,
  CPU, memory, battery and authentication; backgrounds must follow the same rule.

## Verification

The GTK 3 color check passed across configured modules and status classes:
normal backgrounds are `#000000`, and prelight backgrounds are `#161616`
under the black theme. Workspace checks include normal, active, focused and
urgent button classes. A live capture confirms black resting backgrounds
for power and clock. Lint, workspace tests and Spark bridge checks passed.
