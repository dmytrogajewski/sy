# BUG-20261017-2340: Top panel vanishes after packaged apply

## Summary

The Fedora 44 packaged apply left Waybar absent, and a manual restart showed the top bar ending before the right panel with the power icon crowded against the battery.

## Reproduction

- `sy apply` ran from a one-shot user service; no Waybar process remained afterward.
- `systemd-run --user --unit=sy-waybar-recovery --collect /usr/bin/waybar` restored Waybar, but its journal reported width 1892 on a 1920-pixel output.
- The user-provided screenshot showed the right strip above Waybar and the power control adjacent to the battery.

## Expected

Waybar stays visible across applies and logins, spans the top edge, and gives the power control its own space. The right strip begins below it.

## Actual

The apply logic spawned Waybar as a child of a one-shot service, so systemd stopped it with the service. The stack bar reserved 28 pixels with a layer-shell exclusive zone, shortening Waybar even after recovery.

## Root Cause

Waybar had no persistent user unit. Its restart relied on `setsid`, which does not move a process out of its systemd cgroup. The stack bar's right exclusive zone applied across the top edge.

## Fix

Supervise Waybar with `sy-waybar.service`, enable and restart it after unit synchronization, then restart the stack bar so niri positions it below Waybar. Reserve the right strip through niri's layout strut and leave the stack layer-shell exclusive zone at zero. Add spacing and a background to the power control.

## Traceability

- Manual reproduction: `systemctl --user status sy-waybar-recovery.service`, `journalctl --user -u sy-waybar-recovery.service`, and the user's screenshot.
- Fixed in: `crates/sy-desktop-shell/src/{waybar.rs,stack/bar/app.rs}`, `src/main.rs`, `configs/{niri,waybar,systemd/user}`, and `packaging/sy.spec`.
- Verification: packaged apply; `systemctl --user is-active sy-waybar.service sy-stack-bar.service`; Waybar journal width 1920 on eDP-1; visual review.
