# BUG-20261107-0012: Adapter loss leaves a live daemon and a blue key

## Summary

syauth remains running with an obsolete Bluetooth adapter handle, while its
panel reports daemon readiness and authentication cannot reach the phone.

## Reproduction

- Installed packages: syauth 0.1.0-5.fc44, sy-system-auth 0.1.0-23.fc44.
- The process started November 1 and retained its transport until November 7.
- The journal first reports advertisement rotation failures on November 4 at
  17:53, with `target object was either not present or removed`.
- Kernel logs separately record a MediaTek `hci0` coredump on November 6 at
  20:09:46 and initialization of `hci1` at 20:09:53. This later reset does not
  establish the initial cause of the November 4 failure.
- At investigation time BlueZ exposed `hci1`, powered on, with zero active
  advertisements. Authentication requests timed out. The RPC still reported
  `state: up`; the panel emitted `class: ok` and unknown phone availability.

## Expected

Adapter loss invalidates readiness. The daemon reconstructs transport using
the available controller, and the panel distinguishes transport failure from
an ordinary unanswered approval or an absent phone.

## Actual

The rotation loop logs backend errors indefinitely. Startup adapter selection
does not reconstruct a peripheral after its controller disappears. The panel
has only process readiness and authentication history, so it misses this fault.

## Root Cause

The previous repair selects BlueZ's default adapter only at construction.
PersistentPeripheral retains that adapter handle for its lifetime; the
orchestrator logs failed advertisement updates without rebuilding transport.
The status API does not report advertising or transport health independently
of daemon liveness.

## Recovery and Verification

Using the previously authorized service restart, only syauth-presenced was
restarted at 00:12:20 on November 7. The existing bond registered on the current
controller, advertising resumed, and the phone subscribed at 00:12:22.
The challenge at 00:14:07.930 completed with `outcome=ok` at 00:14:10.549.
`timeout 25 sudo -k /usr/bin/true` returned zero through phone approval.

## Fix

The daemon now latches controller removal and probes controller power and active
advertising every two seconds. A failed advertisement clears readiness. A lost
transport or failed Bluetooth startup exits with failure, and the managed service
retries every two seconds without a restart limit. Each restart selects the
available default controller and reconstructs GATT and advertising with the
existing bond. Health probes and orchestrator shutdown have bounded waits.

Status RPC and CLI JSON now report optional `daemon.transport_ready`. The panel
requires reported healthy transport before showing ready, reports known failure
as `Bluetooth transport unavailable · recovering`, and identifies older daemons
that cannot report transport health. An unanswered approval remains request
history when transport is healthy.

## Automated Verification

- A failing startup regression proved that unavailable BlueZ previously left a
  running socket-only daemon. It now exits for supervised recovery.
- A private D-Bus integration test runs the real daemon with BlueZ interfaces,
  removes hci0, verifies failure exit and socket cleanup, and relaunches on hci1.
  Recovery reloads the existing peer and preserves bond and key bytes.
- Controller event tests cover name reuse, unrelated removal, and stream loss.
- Readiness tests cover no-phone advertising, failed rotation, and controller
  loss before the next minute rotation. RPC tests cover legacy wire decoding.
- CLI and applet tests distinguish daemon liveness from transport health.
  The real applet executable shows degraded during a pending challenge when
  transport is broken, while unanswered approval stays normal with healthy
  transport. Missing health reporting cannot produce a ready indicator.

## Deployment

Both native RPM builds passed. Reinstalled syauth 0.1.0-5.fc44 and
sy-system-auth 0.1.0-23.fc44 from the tested source and reloaded the managed
unit. The new daemon started November 7 at 01:36:09, advertised at 01:36:11,
and logged a phone subscription at 01:36:17. Status reports
`transport_ready: true`; BlueZ reports one active advertisement.

A fresh `timeout 25 sudo -k /usr/bin/true` returned zero through phone approval.
The daemon recorded `outcome=ok` for 01:36:33.173–01:36:35.308, taking 2.135
seconds. Package verification passes. SHA-256, ownership, and private modes
match the pre-install snapshot for all seven bond and key files.

`make lint` and `make test` pass in both working trees: sy has 1,751 passing
test results with 12 ignored; syauth has 486 passing results with 10 ignored.
The Spark lint gate and all 18 bridge tests pass. The isolated PR worktree
also passes lint and all 1,670 tests, plus its 17 Spark bridge tests. The syauth dependency checks
include patched `ctutils` and Tokio and the existing informational UniFFI parser
exception. Physical controller removal was exercised on an isolated D-Bus,
while the live host check verified normal advertising, subscription, and approval.
